Bringing back the 90s

Tara, Gimik!


Starting a day with a coffee and a little reminiscing.

We provide a dose of caffeine, roasted with nostalgia of Filipino Pop Culture.


Started in 14th of September 2025, founded by Jesseca Belmin in the heart of Las Piñas City, Metro Manila. As they aimed to serve coffees, they also wanted to serve retro aesthetic to fill the costumers relaxing nostalgia by taking them back to 80's Filipino Pop Culture.


Amidst the rising beige aesthetics of the coffee shops, Gimik Cafe shifted their directions by incorporation 80's jargon "Gimik" that means hangout plans for friendgroups back in the day.

Check out nostalgia by visiting our 80's Hangout Haven!

Ano? Tara gimik!
